# Logos: Fluent family and integrations

Full-colour marks from the plugin's `4-logos-and-integrations` folder, cleaned (titles, ids and inline styles removed) but with their own colours kept.

## How to use
- Use on integration graphics, "works with" rows and app tiles. Show them at their own colours on white or a light palette card; never recolour, outline or redraw a third-party mark.
- Fluent family: `fluentcrm-icon` (purple tile; `fluentcrm-icon-mono-black` is the black version), `fluent-forms`, `fluent-smtp`, `fluent-support`, `fluent-booking`, `fluent-boards`, `fluent-community`, `fluent-connect`, `fluent-cart-dark`, `fluent-toolkit`.
- Integrations: `woo`, `edd`, `wordpress`, `affiliatewp`, `buddyboss`, `grabme`, `csv` (importer).
- Sibling products appear as small tiles only: they never take over a FluentCRM layout, and their colours never become FluentCRM colours.
- An integration shown in marketing must be one FluentCRM really supports; check `tier` and `owner` in the Product Brain before claiming it.


## FluentCRM brand marks (`assets/brand/`)
- `fluentcrm-logo-color`: wordmark with the icon, for light grounds.
- `fluentcrm-logo-white` / `fluentcrm-logo-black`: one-colour wordmarks for purple, dark or very light grounds.
- `fluentcrm-icon`: purple tile with the white F mark (the floating icon tile in illustrations).
- `fluentcrm-icon-inverted`, `fluentcrm-icon-white`, `fluentcrm-icon-black`: alternates.

Third-party marks in logos/ (FluentCart blue tile, WooCommerce, LearnDash via `assets/icons/learndash.svg`) stay at their own colours and appear as small tiles with a name label.
